Changes: display projection matching regions.v1.json; simplification of shared
boundary chains once for both neighbors; finer detail for small areas;
0.01 drawing-unit coordinate rounding; Arabic display labels; reviewed English
name aliases; explicit parent-region labels; interior cartographic label anchors.
These label anchors are not plant observation coordinates.

Geometry is used only to display a named administrative area. Botanical evidence
is assigned from its source's place names, never from observation coordinates.
Shading indicates a place named by the evidence, not occupancy of its whole area.

Limits: generalized community-source boundaries representing 2021, not an
authoritative current Saudi boundary decision. The source has no distinct unit
for Dammam or Al Muwayh; records naming these can fall back to their documented
region. No replacement boundary is invented. Regional-capital areas are marked
isRegionalCapital rather than being silently described as ordinary governorates.

Reproduction: run scripts/prepare-governorates.py with the pinned GeoJSON above.
